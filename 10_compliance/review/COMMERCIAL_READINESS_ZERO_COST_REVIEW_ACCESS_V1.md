## Current legal-regulatory context verified from official sources

1. ANPD — Resolução CD/ANPD nº 2/2022, current official text: agents of treatment of small size can include natural persons that perform personal-data processing while assuming controller/operator obligations. Article 11 states that small agents are not required to appoint a DPO/encarregado, but when no DPO is appointed they must provide a communication channel for data subjects. This flexibility does **not** exempt the agent from the remaining LGPD duties.
   - Source: https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-2-de-27-de-janeiro-de-2022

2. ANPD — Guia Orientativo para Definições dos Agentes de Tratamento e do Encarregado: controller and operator can be natural or legal persons; role classification depends on the actual institutional decision-making over processing.
   - Source: https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/anonimizado___guia_de_agente_de_tratamento_e_encarregado_da_anpd_novo.pdf

These sources are context for the independent reviewer. They do **not** close `LEGAL_ENTITY_IDENTITY`, `CONTROLLER_PROCESSOR_ROLE_MATRIX`, `SENSITIVE_DATA_TREATMENT`, `RETENTION_MATRIX`, `INCIDENT_RISK_AND_COMMUNICATION_PROCEDURE` or any other open decision by themselves.

## Minimal request scope for FitNexus

The review request should ask whether the nucleus can provide preventive/extrajudicial orientation for a pre-launch SaaS operated initially by a natural person, focusing on:

1. supplier/legal identity wording for a PF operator;
2. Terms of Use candidate;
3. privacy/LGPD notice and controller/operator mapping;
4. treatment/minimization of potentially sensitive fitness/health-related data;
5. retention and backup-expiration rules;
6. DSR contact route and incident communication procedure;
7. trial, renewal, cancellation, refund/withdrawal, delinquency and reactivation policy after real billing authority exists.

## Privacy and evidence boundaries

Do not place CPF, residential address, identity documents, reviewer personal data, provider credentials, secrets or raw private consultation records in GitHub. If a real review is accepted, the external working copy may retain the real evidence privately and the repository should record only the minimum non-sensitive authority/digest allowed by the existing intake contracts.

No Supabase mutation, billing activation, Terms publication, deployment, controlled launch or paid media is authorized by this brief.
