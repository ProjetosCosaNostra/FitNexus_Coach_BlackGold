# FitNexus Coach BlackGold — Finance & Tax Autopilot V1

Status: **foundation only — no filing, no tax payment, no accounting/legal attestation**.

This structure exists now so finance and tax operations do not become a last-minute manual burden when revenue starts. The current operator profile is **pessoa física**, using **FitNexus Coach BlackGold** as the commercial brand, with no MEI/CNPJ currently present.

## Operating model

The system is automation-first. It will normalize revenue events, provider fees, refunds, chargebacks and operating costs; reconcile them; prepare monthly-close candidates; calculate only provisional reserves from an externally reviewed rule pack; maintain evidence and due-date candidates; and compare PF versus future formalization options when revenue makes that economically relevant.

The system must never invent tax rules or silently treat a simulation as a filing-ready calculation. Tax rates, deductions, municipal obligations, MEI eligibility and PJ regime assumptions must come from a dated authority pack based on official sources and independent review when needed.

## Formalization gate

Opening a MEI or another CNPJ is not automatic. The gate will compare real revenue stability, current PF obligations, legally available formalization options, fixed compliance cost, estimated tax cost, billing/invoice requirements and a safety margin. A recommendation may be automated; opening or changing the legal form remains a separately authorized action.

## Human work that remains by design

Only actions that cannot safely be delegated remain human: identity verification, government/bank confirmations, legally binding filings, payment approval, contractual/legal review where required and exception resolution when evidence does not reconcile.

## External workspace

The one-command bootstrap creates a private current-only working directory outside the repository. It contains templates for the future transaction ledger, tax-rule authority pack, monthly close, formalization comparison and evidence manifest. Real CPF, bank credentials, provider secrets and government credentials must never be committed here.

## Current commercial effect

This foundation receives **0 percentage points** toward commercial publication. It reduces future operational burden, but the current commercial-readiness percentage changes only when real gate-relevant facts change.
